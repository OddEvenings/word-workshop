Title: Week 13 Vocab 4th Grade

aspire - To have strong hopes to reach a goal.
confidence - A strong, sure feeling about yourself and what you can do.
endeavor - To try very hard to do something.
fearlessness - Not feeling scared; being brave.
elegant - Stylish and graceful.
foreboding - Giving a feeling that something bad is going to happen.
episode - An event or period of time that is important in some way.
scowled - Frowned or made an angry look with your face.
intimidated - Feeling afraid of someone or something.
subdued - Quiet and soft; not strong, loud, or intense.
disheveled - Messy; not neat or tidy.
rigid - Stiff and does not bend easily.
feisty - Bold, full of energy, and determined.
sulked - Was upset about something and refused to talk about it.
