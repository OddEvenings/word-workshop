Title: Week 10 Vocab 4th Grade

surge - A sudden large increase in the depth of water.
perished - Died.
debris - The pieces of something that was broken or destroyed.
adoringly - With a lot of love and admiration.
capable - Having the skill or ability to do something.
spectators - People who watch an event, such as a sports competition.
disbelief - The feeling that something cannot be true.
