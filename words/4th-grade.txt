Title: Example words

habitat - the natural home of a plant or animal
predict - to say what you think will happen next
enormous - very, very big
journey - a trip from one place to another
gather - to bring things together into one group
curious - wanting to know or learn about something
fragile - easy to break
observe - to watch something carefully
swift - moving very fast
delighted - very happy and pleased
