Title: Week 10 Vocab 4th Grade

surge - If there is a surge of water, there is a sudden large increase in its depth.
perished - When people or animals perished, they died.
debris - Debris is the pieces of something that was broken or destroyed.
adoringly - If you act adoringly, you act with a lot of love and admiration.
capable - If a person is capable, he or she has the skill or ability to do something.
spectators - Spectators are people who watch an event, such as a sports competition.
disbelief - Disbelief is not believing that something is true.
