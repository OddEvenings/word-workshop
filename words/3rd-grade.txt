Title: Week 10 Vocab 3rd Grade

independence - Being free to set your own rules and make your own choices.
declaring - Feeling strongly about something and making it clearly known.
endowed - Given a feature or quality that is yours and belongs to you.
presented - Showed or gave something to someone.
gritty - Feels rough and sandy.
broad - Wide.
hoisted - Used ropes to pull a flag up a pole.
