Title: Week 13 Vocab 3rd Grade

audition - A performance you give to show what you can do, often to try out for a part in a play or show.
rehearse - To practice a play, song, or speech many times so that you are ready.
ability - The skill or power to do something because you know how.
actor - A person who performs as a character in plays, movies, or shows.
genuine - Real and exactly what it seems to be.
saga - A long, detailed story about heroic events.
coiled - Shaped into loops or rings.
tame - To teach a wild animal to be gentle and do what you want.
whirled - Spun and turned around very quickly.
