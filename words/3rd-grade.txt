Title: Week 10 Vocab 3rd Grade

independence - If you are free to set your own rules and make your own choices, you have independence.
declaring - When you are declaring something, you feel strongly about it and are making it clearly known.
endowed - If you have been endowed with a feature or quality, it is yours and belongs to you.
presented - If you presented something, you showed it or gave it to someone.
gritty - When something feels gritty, it feels rough and sandy.
broad - Something that is broad is wide.
hoisted - If you hoisted a flag, you used ropes to pull it up a pole.
