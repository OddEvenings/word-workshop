Title: Week 12 Vocab 3rd Grade

ferry - A boat that takes people or vehicles across a river or waterway.
monument - A large statue or building that honors an important person or event in history.
inspired - Made you want to do something.
torch - A long stick with a flame at one end that may be used for light or to start a fire.
sculptor - An artist who uses clay, stone, wood, or metal to make a work of art.
